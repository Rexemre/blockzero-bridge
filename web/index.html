Wrap BLOZ → wBLOZ

  1. Connect your BSC wallet (MetaMask) using the button above.
  2. Click Create deposit address to get a unique BLOZ address (one active per wallet).
  3. Send native BLOZ in one transaction — any amount ≥ 0.01 BLOZ. You receive 96.1% as wBLOZ (3.9% bridge fee).
  4. Do not send twice to the same address. Finish or wait for expiry before creating a new one.
  5. After 6 confirmations, click Claim wBLOZ — you pay a small BNB gas fee.
  6. Claim within 7 days after deposit confirms — otherwise BLOZ is auto-refunded to the sending address (minus network fee).
  7. Add wBLOZ in MetaMask — it will not appear automatically (see section below).

Unwrap wBLOZ → BLOZ

  1. Connect MetaMask on BNB Smart Chain with wBLOZ in your wallet.
  2. Enter the amount and your Block Zero receive address (bz1…).
  3. Approve wBLOZ (first time only) and confirm unwrap — you pay BNB gas.
  4. Native BLOZ is sent to your bz1… address after relayer processing (usually minutes).
  5. Payout = unwrap amount minus 3.9% bridge fee and a small network fee (see below).

Trust & transparency

Every wBLOZ is backed by native BLOZ held in the bridge wallet. Minting happens only through the on-chain claim contract after your deposit confirms.

  • Custodial model: unwrap payouts and auto-refunds are sent from the bridge BLOZ reserve.
  • Claim step: after your BLOZ deposit confirms, it sits in the bridge reserve until you claim wBLOZ on BSC — shown as "awaiting claim" in the live reserves line above.
  • Bridge fee: 3.9% is deducted on wrap (you receive 96.1% as wBLOZ) and on unwrap (you receive 96.1% as BLOZ, minus network fee). The fee stays in the reserve, so wBLOZ remains over-backed.
  • Auto-refund: unclaimed wraps, expired addresses, duplicate deposits, and amounts below the minimum are returned to the original bz1… sender minus the Block Zero network fee.
  • Verify yourself: compare live reserves above with wBLOZ.totalSupply() on BscScan and the bridge wallet balance on the Block Zero explorer.
